How to Request Account Deletion

HeartLink Dating users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by emailing the developer.

  1. Send an email to HeartLinkDatingOfficial@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: HeartLink Account Deletion.
  3. Include the email address associated with your HeartLink account.
  4. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing your request.

Deletion Inside the App

We plan to offer account deletion through: Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account.

Until this feature is implemented and tested, users may contact us by email.

What Information Is Deleted?

After verification, we will process deletion of the account and associated profile data, including profile photos and personal information, subject to applicable retention requirements.

What Information May Remain?

Messages previously delivered to other users may remain visible in those users' conversations.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, disputes, fraud prevention, or safety investigations. Any retention periods and exceptions will be reviewed and disclosed before launch.

Processing Time

We will acknowledge deletion requests and provide information about the expected completion time. Any applicable legal deadlines will be respected.
