1. About HeartLink

HeartLink Dating is a dating application intended for adults aged 18 and older. Its initial launch is planned for The Bahamas and the United States.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, we collect account details, profile information, photos, dating preferences, messages, general location information, and safety reports.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate accounts, display profiles, support matching and messaging, provide safety features, and respond to support requests.

4. Location Privacy

HeartLink is designed to display general locations such as cities, islands, states, and countries, with approximate distances. Users will be able to hide their distance. Exact addresses and live GPS locations are not intended to be publicly displayed.

5. Messaging

Users can exchange messages. Planned deletion features include Delete for Me and Delete for Everyone, without a time limit. Messages already received may remain visible after the sender deletes their account.

6. Firebase and Service Providers

HeartLink uses Google Firebase for application services and data storage. Information may be processed outside a user's home country.

7. Blocking and Reporting

HeartLink plans to provide blocking and reporting tools for harassment, scams, impersonation, and suspected underage accounts.

8. Age Requirement

HeartLink Dating is intended exclusively for people aged 18 or older.

9. Account Deletion

Users will be able to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal data, subject to applicable retention requirements. Previously delivered messages may remain visible to their recipients.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information by contacting us.

11. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as HeartLink's features and data practices change.
